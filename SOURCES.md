# Data Sources

Germany Decoded uses publicly available official sources to build its legal knowledge base.

The current version focuses on German federal legislation, especially the German Civil Code (Bürgerliches Gesetzbuch — BGB).

---

## Gesetze im Internet

Publisher:

Federal Ministry of Justice  
(Bundesministerium der Justiz)

Website:

https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/

Purpose:

Official publication platform for German federal legislation.

Germany Decoded uses this source to:

- retrieve official legal texts,
- extract individual legal sections,
- create searchable documents,
- provide source-grounded answers through the AI assistant.

The source remains the authoritative version of the legislation.

---

## Data Handling

Legal documents are processed locally during the ingestion pipeline.

The repository does not redistribute downloaded legal datasets or store copies of the original source files.

Users generate their own local knowledge base by running the ingestion process.

---

## Future Sources

Future versions may include additional trusted sources, such as:
