# Data Sources

Germany Decoded retrieves information from trusted public sources.

## Gesetze im Internet

Publisher:
Federal Ministry of Justice (Bundesministerium der Justiz)

Website:
https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/

Purpose:
Official publication of German federal legislation.

The project downloads publicly available legal texts during the ingestion step.
The repository does not redistribute downloaded datasets.

The original source remains the authoritative version.

Additional sources may be added in future versions, such as official government guidance and consumer protection organisations.
